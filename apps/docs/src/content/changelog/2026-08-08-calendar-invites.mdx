Meeting invites no longer arrive as a cryptic attachment. Doota reads the invite
and shows a clean **card** — date, time, place (with a **Join** button for Zoom,
Meet, or Teams), who's organizing, and who's going. If you're invited, three
buttons — **Yes**, **Maybe**, **No** — let you respond without leaving your mail.

Doota sends the reply the way each service expects: **Google and Outlook**
invites hand off to their own response flow, while **Apple, Fastmail, and plain**
invites get a proper reply email to the organizer. Either way, only the organizer
hears back, and your reply carries only *your* answer — never the guest list.
Updates and cancellations keep the card in sync, and you're never auto-RSVP'd.
