---
title: Snooze conversations for later
description: Set a conversation aside until a time you choose — it comes back to your inbox then, and a reply always brings it back early.
date: 2026-07-29
tags:
  - feature
---

Not ready to deal with something now? **Snooze** it. The conversation leaves
your inbox and comes back — at the top — at a time you pick: later today,
tomorrow, this weekend, or any exact date and time. You can also just type it
("in 2 days", "next week") or choose it on the calendar.

A **reply always wakes a snoozed conversation early**, so a reply never sits
hidden. From the new **Snoozed** view you can reschedule a wake time or send a
conversation back to your inbox right away.

The same quick picker — one-tap options, plain-language typing, or the calendar —
now powers **Schedule for later**, and it's tuned to work from phones up to large
desktops.
