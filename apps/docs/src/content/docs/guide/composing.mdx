Writing in Doota feels like typing a chat message, but it sends a real email —
with a few handy extras.

## Replying and forwarding

Reply straight in the conversation and it stays threaded, so both you and the
other person see it all in one place. You can also **reply to everyone** on a
group message, or **forward** a message to someone new.

## Drafts save themselves

Started writing but got pulled away? Doota keeps your draft safe. Close it, come
back later, and pick up where you left off — attachments and all.

## Choosing who it's from

If you can send from more than one address — your own, a shared team inbox, or
one of your [extra addresses](/guide/aliases) — Doota lets you pick which one a
message goes out as before you send.

## Undo send

Spotted a mistake the instant you hit send? For a few seconds afterward, an
**Undo** button appears. Tap it and the message is pulled back before it ever
leaves.

## Schedule for later

Pick a date and time — tap a quick option, type it in plain words ("tomorrow
9am", "next week"), or choose it on the calendar — and Doota holds your message
until then. Great for writing now but landing it during work hours. You can
change or cancel a scheduled message any time before it goes.

## Watching it send

After you send, you can see the message move along: _sending_, then _sent_, then
_delivered_ — right there in the conversation, no refresh needed. If a message
can't be delivered (say, a mistyped address), Doota tells you so you're never
left guessing.

<Aside type="note" title="Bounced addresses">
  If an address keeps rejecting your mail, Doota remembers and stops trying, so
  you don't keep firing messages into a dead inbox.
</Aside>
