An **extra address** (an alias) is another email address on your domain that
lands in your mailbox. Think of it as a disposable address: give a different one
to each shop, service, or newsletter. If one starts getting spam, switch it off —
and your real address never got exposed.

You'll find these under your **account settings**.

## How they work

- An extra address like `shopping@yourcompany.com` delivers straight into your
  mailbox, alongside everything else.
- You can see which address a message came in on, so it's easy to tell who's
  been sharing your address around.
- You can also **reply from** an extra address, so the other side only ever sees
  that one — not your real inbox.

## Making one

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your mail settings">
    Go to your account settings and find the addresses section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the address">
    Pick the part before the `@` — say `newsletters` — and Doota makes
    `newsletters@yourcompany.com` for you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Hand it out">
    Use it when signing up for things. Replies you send from it keep your real
    address private.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Switching one off

Turn off an extra address whenever you like. From then on, mail sent to it is
quietly turned away — no bounce-back drama, and nothing new clutters your
mailbox.

<Aside type="tip" title="A tidy habit">
  Use a distinct address per service and you'll always know exactly who leaked
  your email when the spam starts — and shutting it off takes one click.
</Aside>
