Doota's big idea: **conversations, not folders**. Everything with one person (or
group) sits in a single running thread, the way a chat app shows a conversation.
It's still ordinary email, so it works with whatever the other person uses.

## Where mail lives

Instead of hunting through folders, every conversation is in one of a few simple
places:

- **Inbox** — what you're dealing with now.
- **Archive** — done with it, but kept if you ever need it.
- **Spam** — junk.
- **Trash** — deleted, and recoverable for a while.
- **Sent** — conversations you started.
- **Snoozed** — set aside until a time you pick; each returns to your inbox then.

Move a conversation between these anytime. If you share a mailbox with teammates,
each of you organizes it your own way — moving something out of _your_ inbox
doesn't disturb theirs.

## Staying organized

- **Star** a conversation to flag it for later.
- **Snooze** a conversation to clear it from your inbox until a time you choose —
  later today, tomorrow, this weekend, or any date and time (just type "in 2
  days" if that's easier). It returns to the top of your inbox then, and a
  **reply always brings it back early**. From the **Snoozed** view you can
  reschedule it or send it back to your inbox right away.
- **Labels** are your own tags — "travel", "invoices", "family" — to group
  related conversations.
- **Pin** a conversation to keep it at the very top of the list.
- Doota keeps track of what you've read, so unread mail stands out.

Want mail to sort itself? See [Organizing your mail](/guide/organizing) for
**rules** that label, file, or forward incoming messages automatically.

## Finding things

Press **⌘K** (or **Ctrl+K**) to search. Free text finds words in your mail, and
filters like `from:`, `has:attachment`, and `after:7d` narrow it down. Search is
fast because Doota keeps a readable index of your mail — see
[Search](/guide/search) for the filters and what that means for privacy.

Want a heads-up when mail lands? Turn on [notifications](/guide/notifications).

## Sharing an inbox with a team

A mailbox can be shared — handy for something like `support@` or `hello@`. When
it's shared, a conversation becomes a little shared workspace:

- **Assign** a conversation to a teammate so everyone knows who's handling it.
- **Add a private note** that only your team sees — never the person you're
  emailing.
- A quiet history shows who did what (assigned it, moved it), so nothing gets
  dropped.

<Aside type="tip">
  Notes and assignments never reach the person on the other end. They're just
  for your team.
</Aside>

## Is this really from them?

Email addresses are easy to fake, so Doota checks each message against the
sender's own published rules (the same authentication banks and big providers
rely on). When a message checks out, you'll see a green **Verified** badge under
the sender — it means *“this really came from that domain.”* The same badge
shows on the [contact card](/guide/signatures#the-contact-card).

Doota also marks whether a sender is on **your team** or **external**, so a
message that claims to be from a colleague but comes from outside is easy to
spot. A message with no badge simply couldn't be verified — treat it with a
little more caution, especially if it's asking for something.

## Reading a message

Open a conversation and messages read top to bottom, newest at the end. Files
someone sent you show up as attachments you can download — Doota checks each one
first (see [Attachments](/guide/attachments)).
