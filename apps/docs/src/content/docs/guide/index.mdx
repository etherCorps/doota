Doota is email that reads like chat. Every conversation is one running thread,
and it's real email underneath — so anyone on Gmail or Outlook can take part.

New here? Start with [Getting started](/guide/getting-started). Otherwise, jump
to what you need:

## Finding your way around

- **Your mail** is the main screen — your conversations.
- **Your account settings** are where you manage extra addresses, sign-in, and
  your profile.
