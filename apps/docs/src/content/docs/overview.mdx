Doota is email that looks and feels like a chat app. Every conversation is one
simple thread of messages — like texting — but it's real email underneath, so
you can write to anyone on Gmail, Outlook, or anywhere else, and they can write
back.

The name Doota (say _DOO-tah_) means **messenger**.

## What makes Doota different

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Conversations, not folders" icon="ph:chats-circle">
    Each email thread is one running conversation. No inbox archaeology.
  </Card>
  <Card title="It's your address" icon="ph:house">
    Doota runs on your own domain and your own account. No company sits between
    you and your mail.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Private by default" icon="ph:lock-key">
    The contents of your messages are locked up when stored, so a data leak
    can't read them.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Undo send & send later" icon="ph:clock-counter-clockwise">
    Change your mind right after hitting send, or schedule a message for later.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Throwaway addresses" icon="ph:mask-happy">
    Hand out a one-off address for each site. Getting spam? Switch it off.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Simple, strong sign-in" icon="ph:fingerprint">
    Sign in with a passkey (your fingerprint or face) or a password with a
    one-time code.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
